# Supervisory SLM paper → TinyStudio expansion

This maps the supplied 23-page *Supervisory Small Language Models for Hardware Signal Control and System-of-Systems Optimization* to the approved [A–G implementation plan](laboratory-plan.md) and [laboratory requirements](laboratory.md#requirements-and-delivery-map). It is a research traceability document, not a replacement plan, implementation record, or declaration of qualification.

Source PDF SHA-256: `cc88bdca57eff175604273f782dc53cbe1c6825603aa6c04a968adf8220fb06e`. Page references below use the PDF's printed page numbers. The paper's text, tables and text diagrams were reviewed across all 23 pages; its bibliography received the targeted checks described below, not a complete citation audit.

## Scope and interpretation

The paper supplies a concrete research application for the approved workbench: a deterministic controller, a slower model supervisor, and a system-level coordinator. It does not require turning TinyStudio itself into a real-time controller. Fast control and independent enforcement belong on the selected execution target or gateway; the application authors, submits, observes and compares those experiments.

The existing broader portfolio remains in scope: hardware, firmware, physics, energy, IoT, networks, signals, data engineering, scheduling, robotics and interacting agents; language, numerical, multimodal and policy learning; all selected training/retrieval methods; simulation, replay, acquisition, shadow and physical modes; and local/remote/cloud/cluster targets. Solar control is one application, not a new product boundary. A model's failure to beat a conventional baseline remains a valid scientific result.

The paper's instructions, code examples, timing ranges and claimed benefits are research inputs. They do not authorize experiments, physical commands, a new deployment, new paid infrastructure, or progression to E. At this mapping checkpoint, A–C are recorded as accepted in the existing plan; D's final comparison/UI and whole-increment acceptance tasks remain unchecked. E–G remain later acceptance boundaries. Those are the repository's recorded statuses, not fresh requalification by this document.

## Reuse and contract clarifications

The existing [public contracts](../tinystudio/lab/contracts.py) already give this application a place to live: `ExperimentDefinition` contains components, connections, scenarios, clocks, objectives, learning, retrieval, rewards and evaluators; `ResolvedRunSpec` records the submitted research identity and revision; `Episode` separates observations, actions, messages, outcomes and truth evidence. Dataset, retrieval and deployment manifests support lineage and distinct measurements/estimates. These are containers for the application, not evidence that every nested supervisory rule is implemented or validated.

The following details need explicit contracts/fixtures when their owning increment is implemented. They should reuse the existing schemas and plugin boundaries before adding public types or services:

1. **Stateful signal encoding:** dictionary hash; feature window and available-at time; units/scales; entry/exit thresholds; complete treatment of intermediate ranges; elapsed-time versus consecutive-sample dwell; missing/stale behavior; initial and checkpointed encoder state. Hysteresis may suppress chatter while delaying detection, so measure both. Safety detection may require a separate unfiltered path.
2. **Supervisory proposals and enforcement:** controller/parameter version; observation IDs; proposal identity, expiry and ownership epoch; allowed update region/rate; validator identity and result; application/readback evidence; fallback and recovery reason. The paper's illustrative one-field current clamp (p. 16) does not cover malformed payloads, other parameters, dynamics or uncertain outcomes.
3. **Policy envelopes and objectives:** hard constraints versus soft terms, authenticated scope, validity/clock assumptions, priority/conflict rules and offline defaults. Freeze the authored adaptation policy; retain each runtime weight/bounds decision. Authored changes affecting experimental meaning create linked runs, while decisions made by the frozen policy remain events within the run.
4. **Solver supervision:** typed problem and feasibility constraints, solver version/state, seeds, evaluation/time budgets, stopping reasons and reproducible switches. PSO, GA and simulated annealing are candidate custom adapters here, not existing qualifications inferred from generic optimization scope.
5. **Protocol and diary semantics:** protocol variant, byte/word order, scale and register-model revision; transport integrity/status; dictionary compatibility; source timestamps versus receipt times; acknowledgment/outcome semantics; gaps and replay limits. Retain source evidence even when the exchanged summary is lossy.

CANopen, SunSpec mapping, eBPF instrumentation and TimescaleDB-specific acquisition are paper-named integration candidates beyond the plan's explicit named adapter list. Their broad domains fit E's custom extension boundary, but each concrete integration still needs a selection, dependency/capability declaration and qualification fixture. CELI and ELF are research references, not automatic new mandatory backends. This mapping does not adopt them or reopen accepted increments.

## Claims to test rather than adopt

**Hardware sizing.** The paper alternates between MCU and application-processor targets (pp. 1, 5–7, 14). Weight-only storage is `parameters × bits_per_weight / 8`. Wolfram Language evaluation of that expression gives:

| Parameters | Packed four-bit weights, decimal MB | Packed four-bit weights, MiB |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 500 million | 250 | 238.42 |
| 1.5 billion | 750 | 715.26 |
| 3 billion | 1,500 | 1,430.51 |

These are ideal packed-weight calculations, excluding quantization metadata, tokenizer, code, buffers, activations and KV cache. They establish a storage floor, not a target's capacity, accuracy or timing. The cited Deeploy experiment uses a particular small TinyStories Llama configuration and heterogeneous Siracusa MCU with a specific quantization/export path; it does not establish the paper's entire 500M–3B range on generic MCU boards. [Deeploy, §§IV–V](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.04413v1)

**Timing and stability.** A 10–100 kHz loop has a 100–10 microsecond period. A 100 ms–1 s supervisor spans 1,000–100,000 such fast-loop steps, depending on the selected rates; these endpoint calculations were also checked with Wolfram. The paper later describes 10–100 ms ticks (p. 15). Freeze the actual periods, observation age, execution deadlines and fallback budget per experiment. Neither zero latency variance, instantaneous fallback, optimal gains nor zero safety violations is established by this paper's architecture/code examples. A prompt priority and independently bounded parameters are insufficient by themselves to establish stability under switching, delay or state-estimation error.

**Signal representation.** Magnitude, derivative and spectral features are different descriptions of a signal; calling them orthogonal does not establish mathematical independence. Feature loss, aliasing, dwell delay and unavailable future samples need explicit tests. A language model's expanded description cannot recover raw values discarded by a state token. Keep original evidence and label estimates or summaries.

**Optimizer benefit.** The cited LLaMEA identifier `2505.15741` resolves to a different review article. The LLaMEA paper is `2405.20132`, with journal DOI `10.1109/TEVC.2024.3497793`. Its algorithm-generation benchmark is a useful research lead, not proof that a deployed SLM prevents local optima in this control application. [Identifier in the supplied bibliography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15741), [LLaMEA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20132)

**Compression and observability.** RFC 8949 defines CBOR's representation and compactness objectives; it does not establish an 80–95% saving for this workload. Measure `1 - candidate_wire_bytes / baseline_wire_bytes` over matched intervals, including timestamps, dictionaries/schema negotiation, framing, authentication, acknowledgments, retransmissions and reconnect traffic. Compare lossless encodings separately from reduced-rate or lossy state summaries, with a reconstruction/task-error budget. Kernel hooks expose particular software events: the documented BPF socket-lookup hook operates at transport socket selection. Any physical voltage/impedance observation still needs an explicitly capable source adapter. [CBOR objectives, §1.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949.html#section-1.1), [Linux BPF socket-lookup documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/bpf/prog_sk_lookup.html)

**Protocol correctness.** The paper associates a timeout with `MODBUS-ERROR-04` (p. 22). The official specification distinguishes absence of a response/timeout from exception code `0x04`, server device failure. Preserve that distinction in labels, decoders and tests. Register addresses, SunSpec models and cross-protocol command mappings require the actual device/schema revision; the paper's illustrative addresses cannot become generic write rules. [Modbus V1.1b3, §7, pp. 47–49](https://www.modbus.org/file/secure/modbusprotocolspecification.pdf)

**Physical and scientific validity.** The five energy interventions need separate feasible sensing/actuation and physical-model checks. For thermal intervention, account for irradiance, electrical export, heat flow and the energy cost of the action; lower current alone is not evidence of cooling or net yield. Distinguish EMI suppression from recovered electrical energy. Compare row-shading interventions with an appropriate conventional backtracking baseline. Those are proposed discriminating checks, not findings that any intervention works or fails.

**External-framework evidence.** CELI's inspected evaluation concerns programming and content-generation workflows; its HumanEval procedure does not validate hardware control timing or a safety guarantee. The remaining bibliography, including ELF and the paper's other 2026 entries, has not been fully verified here. [CELI, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.14627v1)

## Decisions carried back to the approved expansion

- Keep the A–G dependency order and existing task list unchanged. This mapping creates no new implementation acceptance claim.
- Use the paper as a candidate application of the existing evidence/data/learning foundations and later domain/combined/deployment capabilities.
- Carry the five contract clarifications into their owning increment's architecture review when implementation reaches it; assess schema migration and compatibility then.
- Retain all six interactive views, meaningful 3D inspection, conventional baselines and independent outcome evaluation.
- Keep physical targets, numerical benefits and absolute safety statements explicitly unqualified until appropriate evidence exists. The user selects and conducts research experiments; qualification fixtures establish that the workbench executes and records them correctly.
